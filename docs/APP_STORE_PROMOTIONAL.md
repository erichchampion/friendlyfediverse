## Full description

*Copy into App Store Connect → Description (4,000 characters max). Use the short description above as the first paragraph.*

Friendly Fediverse is a media-centric Mastodon client that defaults to a grid view for quick browsing. Scroll through images and videos at a glance, tap to view full-size, and share or save content for use in other apps—all with a native iOS experience.

**Browse in a grid**

The feed opens in a visual grid by default. Photos, videos, and GIFs from posts appear as thumbnails in a uniform layout—no scrolling past long blocks of text to find media. Switch to list view anytime for the traditional timeline. Your preference is remembered per account.

**Use content elsewhere**

Long-press an image to save or share it to another app. Copy post links, share posts with friends, or send media to your photo library, notes, or creative tools. Friendly Fediverse is built for people who browse to discover and then use what they find.

**Media-first experience**

Images, videos, carousels, and rich link previews are front and center. Smooth video playback with inline controls, image carousels for multi-photo posts, and auto-play for videos and GIFs (optional in settings). High-quality uploads when you compose, so your posts look their best.

**Full Mastodon features**

Connect to any Mastodon instance. Switch between home, local, and public timelines, plus lists and hashtags. Like, boost, bookmark, and reply. Compose posts with photos and videos. Search accounts, posts, and hashtags. Follow and unfollow users. Block, mute, and report when needed. Multi-account support keeps everything in one app.

**Built for performance**

60 FPS scrolling, instant loading with caching, and optimized for battery and data. Light and dark themes with auto (system) support. Full VoiceOver and Dynamic Type support for accessibility.

**Privacy focused**

No tracking or analytics. Your data stays on your Mastodon instance. Open source and transparent—you can see exactly what the app does.

**Who it's for**

Friendly Fediverse is for anyone who loves visual content on the fediverse. Whether you browse for inspiration, collect references, or simply prefer seeing photos and videos first, this app puts media at the center of your Mastodon experience.
