# App Store Listing: v1.0.0

## Promotional Text
Experience the Fediverse natively on iOS. Connect with any Mastodon instance and enjoy a seamless, high-performance social experience with customizable views.

## Description
Welcome to Friendly Fediverse, the premier native client for Mastodon and the broader Fediverse. Built for speed, fluidity, and an intuitive user experience, Friendly Fediverse offers everything you need to connect, share, and discover.

Whether you're exploring your home timeline, catching up on local news, or diving into federated content, Friendly Fediverse delivers it all with stunning native performance.

Key Features:
- **Universal Access:** Securely authenticate and connect with any Mastodon instance.
- **Dynamic Timelines:** Seamlessly switch between Home, Local, Federated, Lists, and Hashtag timelines.
- **Customizable Layouts:** Choose between an immersive grid view or a classic list view to browse your content exactly the way you prefer.
- **Rich Media Experience:** Enjoy smooth inline video playback and swipeable image carousels for multi-media posts.
- **Engage with the Community:** Like, boost, bookmark, and reply to posts effortlessly.
- **Express Yourself:** A fully-featured post composer with media upload support.
- **Discover Content:** Powerful search functionality to find users, hashtags, and interesting posts.
- **Sleek Design:** First-class Dark Mode support and a beautiful, accessible user interface.
- **Reliable:** Offline caching ensures you can read your timelines even when your connection drops.

Join the decentralized social network revolution with a client that feels right at home on your iOS device.
